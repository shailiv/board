ℹ️ How the tags on this board are computed

Everything is extracted deterministically from the posting text — a fixed keyword lexicon and header rules, no AI guessing. A tag can be missing simply because the posting never stated it; postings without a captured description show no tags at all.

Skills are matched from a curated 98-term lexicon and grouped into non-overlapping clusters: SQL & Databases, ETL & Infrastructure, Coding, ML & Statistics, Visualization & BI, Other. Hover any tag for its meaning.

MUST / PLUS badges mirror the posting’s own wording (“a must”, “an advantage”, חובה / יתרון) — absence of a badge means the posting didn’t mark that line. Years is the experience figure stated nearest to “experience”. Degree shows the level and fields asked for; “plus” means the posting itself calls the degree an advantage.

Day-to-day groups classify the responsibilities section: Instrument & manage tracking (Setting up event tracking, taxonomies and instrumentation) · Assure data quality (Validation, anomaly detection, alerting — keeping the data trustworthy) · Run experiments & build models (A/B tests, statistical models and forecasts) · Define metrics & KPIs (Owning metric definitions — the single source of truth for KPIs) · Build pipelines & data models (ETL, warehouse models and analytical infrastructure) · Build dashboards & track performance (Dashboards, recurring reports and performance tracking) · Analyze & recommend (Deep dives and analysis that end in insights and recommendations) · Partner & present (Working with business teams — gathering needs, presenting findings). A chip appears only when a group matches multiple responsibility bullets — it marks an emphasis of the role, not a passing mention — and chips are ordered by how dominant each theme is.

🤖 AI usage classifies what the analyst is expected to do with AI, judged from the words around each AI mention: AI for efficiency (Expected to use AI tools (Copilot, ChatGPT, Cursor…) to work faster) · AI for automation (Using AI to automate processes and workflows) · Building with AI (Building AI/LLM-powered features, models or data products) · AI (unspecified) (AI named as a requirement without a stated purpose). WHERE the mention sits matters: in the requirements section it is prior experience you must bring; in the responsibilities it is part of the job — learnable, not a bar to entry. The dashboard and chips keep the two apart. Mentions of the company’s own AI product (“analyze our AI agents”) are deliberately NOT counted — that is product analysis, not AI usage.

Soft skills (dotted chips) are tagged from the requirements section only — the person the posting describes, separate from the toolbox: communication, ownership, business acumen, curiosity, and so on. Hover any chip for its meaning.
